## Optional model assets

Model weights are not shipped in this repository or bundled in the app. Apple speech assets are installed and managed through Apple's Speech framework. Optional diarization assets are fetched through an explicit setup action from the [FluidInference speaker-diarization model repository](https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml).

The current adapter requests the legacy `pyannote_segmentation.mlmodelc` and `wespeaker_v2.mlmodelc` assets. The upstream repository's newer Community-1 license/provenance confirmation explicitly excludes these legacy names. Accordingly, this project does **not** claim that its downloaded legacy assets are covered by that confirmation or that a blanket CC-BY-4.0 attribution completes their license review. Asset-specific source, terms, attribution, and distribution suitability remain a release gate. See the [upstream scope clarification](https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml/commit/df2625ac79a7ac6b65ad868fee6d80f320da4232) and [provenance record](https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml/blob/main/PROVENANCE.md).

Do not redistribute model weights as part of a release until the exact asset/revision terms are verified. Keeping downloads separate is not a substitute for resolving that provenance. macapy's MIT license does not grant rights to third-party models or imply endorsement by their authors.

## Speech and evaluation fixtures

The public source tree contains original synthetic text fixtures under the repository MIT license. Speech WAVs from the earlier development baseline have been removed from the current tree: recorded provenance identifies Apple System Voices for some files, and the remaining legacy files lacked established redistribution provenance. The [macOS Tahoe software license, section 2F](https://www.apple.com/legal/sla/docs/macOSTahoe.pdf) restricts System Voices content to personal, noncommercial use and excludes public sharing. The exact historical files were preserved locally; older Git objects may still contain them and no history rewrite is implied.

Real speech/diarization tests now require explicitly supplied local fixtures. The optional generator is for personal local use with already-installed voices; generated WAVs must not be published. The committed schedule describes the historical synthetic two-voice test; a newly generated fixture uses its own matching local schedule. See the [development guide](guide/development.md).
